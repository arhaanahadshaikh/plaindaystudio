Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Covers all applications published by Plain Day Studio

Overview: Plain Day Studio ("we", "us", or "our") builds applications for both Android and Apple iOS platforms. Our portfolio uses a hybrid approach: several apps (such as Do Nothing, Breathing, Stare, Pop, and Wait) operate completely offline with zero user accounts and zero telemetry. For collaborative apps requiring synchronisation (such as SwimPulse Pro and SplitPlain), we collect phone numbers or email addresses strictly for authentication and data sync. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Developer Identity

This Privacy Policy governs all applications, websites, and beta testing programs provided by Plain Day Studio.

2. Information We Collect

A. Offline-Only & Account-Free Apps

For our standalone utility and mindfulness applications—including Do Nothing, Breathing, Stare, Pop, Wait, and offline modes of One Thing and PhoneDown:

  • No Login Required: You do not need to register an account, phone number, or email address.
  • Local Storage: Your preferences, streaks, and timestamps are stored exclusively on your device hardware (using SQLite, CoreData, or device preferences). This data is never sent to our servers.

B. Cloud-Enabled & Collaborative Apps

For applications offering multi-device synchronization, team sharing, or expense splitting (such as SwimPulse Pro, SplitPlain, and cloud-synced accounts):

  • Account Credentials: We collect your Phone Number (via SMS verification OTP) and/or your Email Address to create and secure your user account.
  • Profile Details: Optional display name, avatar, or role (e.g. Swimming Coach vs. Parent in SwimPulse Pro).
  • User-Generated Content: Swim lap records, meet times, shared expense entries, participant names, and group balances. This data is securely stored in encrypted cloud databases to sync across your devices and invited collaborators.

C. Diagnostic & Crash Information

When an application experiences an unexpected crash, Google Play Console or Apple App Store / TestFlight may transmit anonymized technical crash logs (such as device manufacturer, operating system version, and stack traces). These logs do not contain your personal information.

3. Payments & Subscriptions

Some of our applications offer premium features or recurring subscription plans (e.g., team rosters in SwimPulse Pro).

All financial transactions are handled exclusively by:

  • Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit): On iOS devices.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: On Android devices.

Plain Day Studio never collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. Billing terms, renewals, and refunds are managed directly through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

4. Device Hardware Permissions

Depending on the specific app, we may request the following permissions:

  • Sensors & Accelerometer (PhoneDown): To detect when your phone is placed face-down on a flat surface. Sensor data is processed entirely in memory on your device and is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Notifications: To deliver scheduled habit reminders, timer alerts, or coach updates. You can revoke this permission anytime in your device settings.
  • Foreground Service & Audio: To sustain audio playback or vibration cues while your screen is off (e.g., in Breathing).
  • Network Access: Only requested by apps that sync data to the cloud (such as SwimPulse Pro and SplitPlain). Offline apps do not request network access.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play User Data policies, users have full control over their data:

  • Local Data: You can purge all local data anytime by tapping "Clear Data" in your device app settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly inside the settings of any cloud-connected app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or by emailing contact.plaindaystudio@gmail.com with your registered phone number/email. All account records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. In applications like SwimPulse Pro where minor athletes' swimming times may be tracked, such records must be entered directly by an authorized parent, guardian, or coach who holds parental consent.

7. Beta Testing Notice

All applications currently featured on our website are in Beta Testing Phase. If you join our Google Play Closed Testing or Apple TestFlight programs, diagnostic logs, feedback submissions, and bug reports will be used solely to improve product stability prior to general availability.
