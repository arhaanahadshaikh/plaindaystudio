Frequently Asked Questions

How do I test on iOS (Apple TestFlight)?

Download the official Apple TestFlight app from the App Store. When you submit your Apple ID email above, you will receive an invitation email with a redeem code or direct test link.

How do I test on Android (Google Play)?

We add your Google account to our closed testing track in Google Play Console. You will receive an opt-in web link that unlocks the private beta on your device's Google Play Store app.

Which apps require a login?

Only multi-device collaborative apps: SwimPulse Pro (for swim teams & parents) and SplitPlain (for group expenses) use phone number or email authentication. Mindfulness tools stay 100% account-free and offline.

How do subscriptions work?

During beta testing, pro features are free. When launched, pro subscriptions will be billed via official Apple StoreKit or Google Play In-App Billing, allowing easy cancellation in your phone's OS settings anytime.
