Studio Principles

Built Right, Priced Fairly

How Plain Day Studio approaches software craftsmanship, user attention, and privacy.

Native Android & iOS Flow

Built for high frame rates, responsive gesture systems, and native system haptics across both Google Android and Apple iOS platforms.

Hybrid Privacy Model

Mindfulness apps run completely offline with zero accounts. Collaborative apps (SwimPulse Pro & SplitPlain) use phone/email authentication with encrypted sync.

No Predatory Traps

Zero forced 30-second full-screen video ads. Core utilities are free forever. Pro subscriptions process transparently through official Google & Apple billing.
